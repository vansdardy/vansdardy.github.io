## What it is

This paper analyzes two arguments on whether or not Hayek had committed naturalistic fallacy
in justifying his economic philosophy on spontaneous order.
The paper examines the arguments made by Angner and Caldwell, and determines which argument
more accurately interprets Hayek's writings and thoughts.
It concludes that neither side had rigorously justified their claims, with certain
imperfections in their arguments.
